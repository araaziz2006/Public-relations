ABOUT OUR DEPARTMENT

History and About Our Department

The Public Relations Department was established in 2021 to provide academic personnel and fulfill market requirements. This department places equal emphasis on both theoretical and practical aspects. Due to the importance of public relations in both private and public sectors, practical tools and methods are utilized to produce professional PR specialists. Full attention is given to subjects essential for today's market demands, such as political advisory, institutional strategic consultancy, innovation, strategy formulation, branding, consumer behavior research, reputation management, campaign management, and crisis prevention.

Why Did I Create This Website?

When I finished 12th grade, I searched extensively for a major that matched my passion, interest, and experience. PRACTICAL LEARNING

🏆 Successful Campaign of the Month: "Share a Coke"

How Public Relations (PR) science drives global success for major corporations?

The Historic Coca-Cola Campaign — "Share a Coke with..."

In 2011, Coca-Cola faced declining sales among young consumers. Instead of running traditional product ads, the PR team decided to replace the main brand logo on bottles with **250 popular first names**!

💡 The PR Secret to Its Success:

The company didn't just sell a drink; it **created a personal emotional connection**. People shared photos of personalized bottles across social media. Consequently, over **500,000 photos** were shared online, boosting Coca-Cola's sales by **2.5% for the first time in years**.

Key Takeaway: The power of Public Relations lies in shifting public behavior and perception seamlessly and organically!
