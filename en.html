About Our Department

History and About Our Department

The Department of Public Relations was founded in 2021 to prepare academic cadres and meet the growing demands of the market. The department emphasizes both theoretical and practical dimensions. Given the importance of Public Relations across private and public sectors, tools and techniques are utilized to foster professional PR specialists. Special emphasis is placed on modern market-driven subjects such as political advisory, organizational strategic consulting, innovation, strategy formulation, branding, consumer behavior research, reputation management, campaign execution, and crisis management.

Creator's Note

Why Did I Create This Website?

When I finished 12th grade, I searched extensively for a department that matched my passion, interests, and background. Among several options, Public Relations caught my attention the most — but since it was a newly established department at the University of Sulaimani, there was very little information on social media or the internet regarding study details, course contents, and job prospects.

Once I entered university and experienced the coursework, learned from accomplished professors and doctors, and particularly gained insights from our department head who possessed immense knowledge about the field, I realized I had made the right choice. Therefore, I built this website for students so that they wouldn't face the same lack of information I experienced and could gain a clear, comprehensive understanding of this department.

— Ara Aziz

Practical Case Study

🏆 Campaign of the Month: "Share a Coke"

How Public Relations science generates global market success for elite multinational brands.

The Landmark Coca-Cola Campaign — "Share a Coke with..."

In 2011, Coca-Cola faced declining sales trends among young adult demographics. Instead of launching traditional product advertisements, the PR strategy team temporarily replaced the iconic logo on bottles with **250 popular first names**!

💡 The Strategic PR Secret:

The brand stopped selling just a soft drink and started creating **emotional personal connections and social sharing**. People spontaneously shared over **500,000 photos** online featuring bottles with their names or friends' names, driving a **2.5% increase in total sales for the first time in over a decade**.

Key Takeaway: The power of PR lies in its ability to shape consumer behavior organically, building trust without making audiences feel overtly sold to!

Quick Answers

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, factual responses to the most common queries raised by prospective students.

1. What language is used for instruction in the Public Relations Department?
Core modules are taught primarily in Kurdish, supplemented by key academic English terminology and dedicated English language courses to strengthen student language proficiency.
2. Is admission open to Literary high school branch students only, or Scientific branch too?
Graduates from both Literary and Scientific branches can apply through the official Zankoline or Parallel admission channels.
3. What is the core distinction between "Public Relations (PR)" and "Media / Journalism"?
Media/Journalism focuses heavily on news reporting and broadcasting operations; Public Relations is the strategic management of organizational reputation, crisis control, strategic planning, and building trust between institutions and their stakeholders.
